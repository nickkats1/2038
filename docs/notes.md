# Notes

this will cover why I wrote major parts of the story.

## Inspirations

I wanted to write a novel on the idea that people get paid for currating prompts for Reinforcement Learning for Human FeedBack. I saw that there were many other "skills" with these listings and that is what inspired me to write this book. I am not a massive fans of novels but I love the novel "1984". It has been my favorite novel for decades for no real reason. Winston Reed, is a obvious reference to Winston Smith from "1984". I wanted this novel however to not be preachy or "nightmare-ish". I did not want any great message (I do not have one) and I went with "What if LLMS litterally automate everything, what would society and the Economics of that society be like"? So, I though, "well, llms need RLHF of some kind (yes, GRPO and DPO). So, I just decided "make everyone in this book select being prompts and make it so they do it for stuff they like or have put a enourmous amount of time into".

## Naming Conventions

I did not want my novel to have name for things with anything that was real. The transistors are inspired by LLM's.

### Why I wrote them as transistors

I remembered the Beatles Movie: "A Hard Days Night" and I remembered the scene with all of the Beatles on a train with the old man. They were playing early 60s/late 50s rock and roll on a transistor. I literally just named the main thing driving society in this book after that scene in the movie and nothing else.

### Why I wrote this in England?

I am a American(Greek-American). I have never been to England. I wrote this in a fictional city because I felt if I wrote it in the perspective of a American city it would be too political or over-sensationalized (really that is unavoidable in this country). I do not like politics at all and AI is not political. It is just math. I grounded it in England and I made it authentic based off of lots of Rolling Stones, Beatles, ect interviews I watched since I was about ten years old, The TV show: Skins, who, I named one of the main characters after, TrainSpotting and more. I did not want this to be Grimey like LiverPool, I wanted it to be a city outside of LiverPool. Where, they do not sound scouse, but have a accent that is very close.

### Why I Avoided dark tones.

I do not like novels for this reason. There does not always have to be a "dark" aspect of human nature. There is no perfect way the world should be, yet there are the laws that govern man. These laws are rational. The transistors are rational. I did not want this to be like "1984" or "A Brave New World". In wanted to avoid having any sort of message at all cost, because I do not have one and I love the transformer and I love LLM's.

### The Mags

So, robotics is no where close to being where AI is at in terms of training data that is digital. i avoid making them `robots` because really, I do not think any mechanism will come around the scales like the transformer. I made it so mags were pretty much decent robots that all had a API call to a transistor and could call a vector database (or something) to always optimize for their owner.


### The Owners

If the owners were to be compared to real life figures, they are people who own LLMS, massive ones. The CEO's. I did not want the novel to paint a cartoonish villain. That is too American and even in reality, they are not really villains. Any single time a new technology has came along, it was not really a moral stance to use it or not, people always did and always will.

### The names of the characters.

- **Cassie May**: Named after Cassie from the TV show `skins`
- **Sterling Bronson**: Combined `Sterling Archer` and `Bronson` from the movie `Bronson`.
